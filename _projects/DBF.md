*MECH.4230 Capstone Design, UMass Lowell<br>*
*Fall, 2024*

For my Capstone design class, my team designed a remote control airplane for the AIAA's annual Design, Build, Fly competition. I was responsible for designing the electronic propulsion system (motor, battery, speed controller, propeller), designing custom 3D printed structural components, and developing the manufacturing process for the airplane.

## Design

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/DBF/plane_cad.png){:width="80%"}{: .align-center}

## Testing Motor Thrust, and Control Surface Servos

Tested thrust of propeller and motor using two 20kg load cells and AC ammeter.

Converted mv/V readings to N, to ensure sufficient thrust (targeted ~70% of plane weight)

Tested for power draw, and battery life at multiple throttles to ensure sufficient flight time.

## Manufacturing

Developed the manufacturing processes, lasercut the chassis from foam/cardboard, and built the entire airplane. I also 3D printed spar connectors, and control surface mounts out of LDPLA.
